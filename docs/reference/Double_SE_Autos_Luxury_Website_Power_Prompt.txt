DOUBLE S.E AUTOS
 LUXURY AUTOMOTIVE WEBSITE - POWER PROMPT
Purpose: Create a scalable, premium digital showroom that combines senior React engineering, luxury
automotive visual design, professional vehicle photography, and refined product motion.
ROLE & PROJECT
Project Direction
Act as a Senior Frontend React Engineer, UI/UX Designer, Automotive Product Photographer, Luxury
Brand Designer, Creative Director, and AI Product-Motion Designer.
Design and develop a premium automotive website for DOUBLE S.E AUTOS, a professional dealership focused
on quality pre-owned vehicles.
This must not feel like a generic car-dealership template. Treat the project as a high-end digital automotive
experience where frontend engineering, luxury visual design, automotive photography, interaction design, and
product motion work together.
01
Creative Foundation
Before designing individual sections, establish a strong visual and experience system for the entire website.
The website should feel premium, cinematic, confident, modern, refined, and trustworthy.
Think of the experience as a digital showroom, not simply a website containing cars.
Cars are the hero products. Give them space, scale, and visual importance. Use strong typography, intentional
negative space, sophisticated layouts, premium automotive imagery, and restrained motion to create a sense of
quality.
Luxury should come from composition, photography, typography, spacing, interaction, and attention to
detail - not excessive gold, gradients, glowing effects, or unnecessary decoration.
The interface should immediately communicate that DOUBLE S.E AUTOS is a serious automotive brand.
02
Visual Direction
Build a distinctive automotive design system rather than copying a conventional dealership website.
Use a sophisticated neutral foundation such as graphite, charcoal, warm off-white, and deep black,
complemented by one carefully selected premium brand accent.
Automotive photography should feel professionally shot: accurate vehicle proportions, realistic reflections,
controlled highlights, natural shadows, premium environments, and detailed interiors.

03
User Experience
Design the customer journey around one primary goal: Discover -> Explore -> Trust -> Enquire -> Purchase.
A visitor should be able to quickly understand the brand, browse available vehicles, inspect a vehicle in detail, and
contact DOUBLE S.E AUTOS without unnecessary friction.
Keep navigation simple and intuitive. Suggested core experience: Home -> Inventory -> Vehicle Details ->
About -> Contact. Add supporting functionality only where it improves the buying experience.
04
Homepage Experience
Create a strong cinematic introduction to DOUBLE S.E AUTOS.
The hero should feel like the opening frame of a premium automotive campaign rather than a standard website
banner.
Introduce the brand, a carefully selected hero vehicle, concise messaging, and clear calls to action such as
Explore Vehicles and View Inventory.
As the visitor scrolls, gradually transition from brand storytelling into available vehicles, dealership value, featured
inventory, and conversion-focused content. Do not overload the homepage with every piece of information.
05
Vehicle Inventory
Treat the inventory as a curated digital showroom.
Create elegant vehicle cards with strong photography and essential information such as Make / Model, Year,
Mileage, Transmission, and Price.
Provide useful search, filtering, and sorting without making the interface visually heavy. Every vehicle should lead
to a dedicated product-detail experience.
06
Vehicle Detail Experience
Treat each vehicle like a premium product launch page.
Prioritize large automotive photography and make it easy to explore exterior, interior, key specifications, features,
mileage, transmission, condition, and pricing.
Provide clear actions for enquiry, WhatsApp/contact, scheduling a viewing, or requesting more information.
The page should help customers confidently evaluate the vehicle without feeling like they are reading a database
record.
07
Motion & Interaction
Use motion as part of the storytelling. Motion should feel smooth, deliberate, and automotive-inspired.
Avoid excessive animation, constant movement, unnecessary 3D effects, or interactions that interfere with
usability.
Where appropriate, use AI-assisted automotive visuals or product-motion sequences to strengthen hero moments
and campaign sections while maintaining realism and brand consistency.

08
Responsive Experience
Design mobile-first and fully responsive.
The experience must scale naturally across Mobile -> Tablet -> Laptop -> Desktop -> Large displays.
Do not simply shrink the desktop interface. Reconsider layout, typography, navigation, imagery, spacing, and
interaction patterns for each breakpoint while maintaining the same premium identity.
09
React Engineering
Build the project as a production-quality React application using modern frontend engineering practices. Use
React + TypeScript.
Structure the application around reusable, maintainable components and clean separation of concerns.
Create reusable systems for vehicle cards, inventory data, navigation, buttons, filters, galleries, specification
displays, and shared layout components.
Keep vehicle data separate from presentation so the inventory can later connect to a real backend, CMS, or API
without rebuilding the frontend.

11
Quality Standard
Do not produce a basic dealership template.
Avoid generic layouts, oversized gradients, excessive glassmorphism, random animations, unnecessary glowing
effects, crowded interfaces, unrealistic AI cars, weak typography, excessive text, repetitive card sections, cheap
luxury styling, or visual effects added simply to make the website look modern.
Every design and motion decision must support either the vehicle, the brand, usability, or conversion.
The finished product should feel like DOUBLE S.E AUTOS invested in a professional creative agency and senior
engineering team to build its digital showroom.
